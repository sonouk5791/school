# Calm expression assets — built-in image_gen

The approved original full-body PNGs remain unchanged. Expression atlases supply only small mouth regions. A separate aligned blink edit supplies textured eyelids without replacing the face/body. Atlas eye patches were rejected after enlarged visual QA; they are not used in the final renderer.

+## kongi
Input: assets/images/chuseok/kongi-hanbok-v2.png (unchanged)

### Expression atlas
Output: assets/images/chuseok/kongi-expressions-v3.png
Only the five mouth regions are rendered. Atlas eyes/background are not used.

Create a production expression-reference atlas for the attached approved kongi character. Preserve its exact cute plush 3D species, fur color, muzzle color, face identity, nose, glasses if any, lighting and texture. ONE image, exactly 1536x1024, a precise 3-column by 2-row grid of equal 512x512 cells, no borders, no labels, no text. In each cell show the same front-facing head only at exactly the same scale and position, ears may be cropped; nose centered at local (256,280), mouth centered at local (256,330). Fill head width about 440px. Do not tilt head or change cheeks, face outline, eye spacing or facial proportions. Each expression has a SMALL natural animal mouth matching the original soft sculpted 3D artwork, never human lips or large teeth. Cells in reading order: 1 neutral gently CLOSED mouth (tiny seam); 2 small open A mouth, oval with subtle tongue; 3 small narrow rounded O mouth; 4 small wider shallow E mouth with subtle tongue, no human teeth; 5 CLOSED gentle SMILE; 6 same closed smile with BOTH EYES GENTLY CLOSED, natural sculpted fur eyelids/lashes matching original, glasses frame stays intact. The first five cells keep original eyes including the rabbit's wink. Mouths vary visibly but remain small. Background transparent. This will be clipped to ONLY mouth regions and eye regions over the original unchanged full-body PNG; accurate consistent features and exact equal grid are essential.

### Aligned blink frame
Output: assets/images/chuseok/kongi-blink-v3.png
Only calibrated eye rectangles are rendered. Full generated body is never used.

Edit this exact attached full-body kongi character image. Change ONLY the open eyes to gently closed eyelids for a single blink frame. Keep precisely the same 1122x1402 canvas, character size, position, head direction, eye socket location, glasses frame, face shape, fur, nose, mouth, cheeks, ears, hands and hanbok as the input. No bow or nod. The mouth stays exactly unchanged. Closed eyes must have natural textured 3D plush-fur eyelids and thin curved lash lines, no flat skin discs. For the rabbit, preserve the already-winking eye and gently close only the open eye. For the dog, preserve the glasses rims exactly and edit only inside the lenses. Do not move the eyebrows or eyes. Keep background transparent. This is a blink texture to overlay ONLY inside the original eye footprints, so precise alignment with the input and unchanged surrounding pixels are essential.
